Problem Statement: “The Coordination Problem”

On a mid-sized college campus, many day-to-day activities depend on people coordinating with one another and sharing resources.

Students often face issues such as:

Not knowing the latest status of something they depend on

Acting on outdated information or missing updates

Manual coordination through calls, messages, or spreadsheets

Confusion about responsibility, ownership, or availability

Administrators and staff often struggle with:

Tracking what is happening vs. what was planned

Knowing who is responsible for what

Handling last-minute changes

Getting reliable data instead of assumptions

These challenges result in wasted time, frustration, and inefficiency.

Your Task

Based on the above situation:

Identify one concrete problem you believe is worth solving

Decide which part of the system you want to design and build

Design and implement a solution for it

We will not prescribe:

What entities to create

What APIs to expose

What tables to design

What features to implement

Choosing what to build — and what not to build — is a key part of this exercise.

Questions & Clarifications

You may ask clarification questions during the assignment window. We can clarify the problem context and validate assumptions, but we will not provide designs, schemas, or step-by-step guidance. Think of this as a real-world product problem where ambiguity is part of the challenge.
